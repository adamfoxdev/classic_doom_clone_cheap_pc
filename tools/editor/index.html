    Tips

    Heights: ceilings go from 1 to 10 units, floors from 0 to 8.75. You can walk up a rise of 0.5 and jump about 0.9 more; anything taller needs the jetpack (J). Ledges then count as reachable in the checks. Checkpoint pads (+) claim the ledge they sit on; the lift pad (=) returns you to the highest one reached. Portal digits only link maps in the built-in hub. Your work is kept in this browser between visits, but Save writes the real file.
